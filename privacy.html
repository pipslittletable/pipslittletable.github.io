Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Scope

This policy covers Pips Yayin, the internal tool of Pip's Little Table. Pips Yayin connects only to our own YouTube channel (@pipslittletableTV). It does not connect to anyone else's account and is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic information about our own channel: name, ID, subscriber and video counts.
  • Information about our own videos: titles, publish dates, view and like counts, and audience settings.
  • Aggregate YouTube Analytics reports for our own channel, such as views, watch time and average view duration per video.

Data we do not access

Pips Yayin does not read, store or reply to comments, and does not access any personal information about viewers. Our channel is set as made for kids, and we do not collect personal information from children.

How we use it

Only to understand how our own videos perform, so we can make better episodes. Data is never sold, rented, used for advertising, shared with third parties, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on a private server in an access-restricted file. Statistics are kept only as aggregate numbers about our own videos.

Google and YouTube data

Pips Yayin uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy. Pips Yayin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of data, email us; requests are handled within 30 days.
